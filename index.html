Drawn to match the diagram on your fuse box lid · tap any slot

Reading the lid: small outlines are short mini blade fuses; large outlines are tall cartridge (J-Case) fuses; the biggest are bolt-in links. A slashed dashed box is an unused slot. Everything here follows the lid photo, which differs slightly from some online lists. The lid says to use the designated fuse only, so confirm against your lid and owner's manual before replacing anything, and always match the amp rating and type.
